What Knight School solves

English can be correct and still not work.

The gap is often not grammar alone. It can be confidence, structure, tone, timing, exam strategy, or uncertainty about how a message will land.

01

Know what to say

Build clear structures for answers, meetings, interviews and difficult conversations.

02

Know how to say it

Practise tone, fluency, vocabulary and delivery without forcing unnatural “perfect English.”

03

Know why it works

Understand the social meaning behind communication so you can adapt independently.

The method

Human first. Evidence-minded. Practical.

Knight School uses psychology as a diagnostic lens, not as a label. Lessons focus on observable communication problems and usable strategies: reduce unnecessary pressure, model the target, practise deliberately, and transfer the skill into real life.

The coaching loop
1
Diagnose
Separate a knowledge gap from confidence, fluency, structure or strategy.
2
Practise
Use targeted examples, guided rehearsal and meaningful feedback.
3
Transfer
Leave with a repeatable move the learner can use without the teacher.
